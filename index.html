How to Verify Safely
  • 1
    Do not click links or buttons — If the message claims your account has an issue, open a new browser tab and navigate to the official website manually.
  • 2
    Never disclose credentials — Legitimate support teams will never ask for your password, PIN code, 2FA code, or full credit card number over email, SMS, or chat.
  • 3
    Verify through an independent channel — Look up the company's verified customer support number or portal directly from their official site, never from the message itself.
  • 4
    Examine sender details carefully — Scammers frequently substitute letters with numbers (e.g., zero for "o") or use free email services posing as corporate support.
  • 5
    Report and block the sender — Forward phishing emails to your email provider or company fraud division (e.g. stop-spoofing@amazon.com, spoof@paypal.com).
  • 6
    Pause before reacting to urgency — Threatening immediate account closure or legal consequences is the #1 tactic scammers use to prevent careful verification.
